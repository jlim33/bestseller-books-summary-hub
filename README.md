# 🌿 VitaPulse (비타펄스)
### Evidence-Based Health, Nutrition & Longevity Intelligence Hub

*Real-time health intelligence platform powered by peer-reviewed research (The Lancet, Nature Medicine, Harvard Health), pastel calming UI, and relaxing ambient music lounge.*

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **🔬 Peer-Reviewed Clinical Evidence**: Strict sourcing from The Lancet, Nature Medicine, JAMA, Harvard Health, and PubMed to minimize AI hallucination.
2. **📊 Evidence Level Grading**: Meta-Analyses, RCTs, and Clinical Cohort studies with safety & dosage guidance.
3. **🎵 Relaxing Audio Lounge**: Classical Piano (Chopin, Bach), Parisian Chanson, 528Hz Deep Meditation & Nature Rain.
4. **🎨 Pastel Healing Aesthetic**: Soothing Sage Green, Lavender, Warm Peach, and Sky Blue colors.
5. **🎙️ US Native Medical Voice TTS**: Calming American medical broadcast voice briefing.
6. **🌐 Dual Language Architecture**:
   - 🇰🇷 Korean Edition: `/` (비타펄스)
   - 🇺🇸 Global English Edition: `/en` (VitaPulse)
